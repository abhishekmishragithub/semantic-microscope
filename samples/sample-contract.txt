This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of 1 March 2026 (the "Effective Date") by and between Halcyon Data Systems, Inc., a Delaware corporation with its principal place of business at 4410 Pier Avenue, Suite 900, Oakland, California 94607 ("Provider"), and Meridian Logistics Group Ltd., a company incorporated in England and Wales with company number 09876543 and its registered office at 18 Coldharbour Lane, London SE5 9PR ("Customer"). Provider and Customer are each referred to herein as a "Party" and together as the "Parties".

RECITALS

Provider operates a cloud-hosted freight visibility and predictive scheduling platform marketed under the name Halcyon Orbit. Customer operates a multimodal logistics business across the United Kingdom, the European Union and the Middle East and wishes to procure access to the platform together with related implementation and support services. Provider is willing to provide such services on the terms and subject to the conditions set out in this Agreement. The Parties have negotiated this Agreement at arm's length and each has had the opportunity to obtain independent legal advice. Accordingly, in consideration of the mutual covenants set out below, the Parties agree as follows.

S2.1 Provider shall make the Platform available not less than 99.9 percent of the time in each calendar month, measured at the boundary of Provider's hosting environment and excluding planned maintenance notified in accordance with Clause 2.3 ("Availability"). "Downtime" means any period of at least five consecutive minutes during which the Platform's core shipment tracking and scheduling functions are unavailable to all Authorised Users, as measured by Provider's monitoring systems. Where Availability is below 99.5 percent but at least 99.0 percent, the service credit shall be ten percent. Where Availability is below 99.0 percent but at least 98.0 percent, the service credit shall be twenty percent. S2.3 Provider shall classify each support request by severity in consultation with Customer. A Severity 1 incident is one in which the Platform is unavailable or a core function is unusable for all Authorised Users and no workaround exists. A Severity 2 incident is one in which a core function is materially degraded, or is unavailable for a significant subset of Authorised Users, and no reasonable workaround exists. A Severity 3 incident is one in which a non-core function is impaired or a reasonable workaround exists. A Severity 4 request is a question, cosmetic defect or enhancement request. Where the Parties disagree on the severity of an incident, Customer's reasonable classification shall apply pending resolution of the disagreement.

S2.4 Provider shall acknowledge a Severity 1 incident within fifteen minutes and shall work continuously on a resolution or workaround until the incident is resolved, providing updates to Customer at least every sixty minutes. Provider shall acknowledge a Severity 3 incident within four business hours and shall provide a resolution or workaround within five Business Days. Provider shall acknowledge a Severity 4 request within one Business Day and shall address it in a future release at Provider's discretion. Severity 1 and Severity 2 support shall be available twenty-four hours a day, seven days a week.
